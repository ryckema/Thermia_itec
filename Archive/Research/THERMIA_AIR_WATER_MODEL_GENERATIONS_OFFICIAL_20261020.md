# Thermia air-to-water generations — official product chronology and research implications (2026-10-20)

**Classification:** official Thermia product/documentation research plus separately labeled hypotheses; **not a live XTR experiment**.

Official Thermia Sweden history: ATEC launched **2011**; inverter iTec **2017**; iTec Eco R32 **2020**; premium Athena **2021**; iTec XT **2024** in historical milestone (Belgian announcement dated **2023-12-01**); iTec XTR R290 **2025**. Thermia's 2018 manufacturer equivalence statement says ATEC corresponds to Danfoss DHP-AQ (external panels/cabinet differences), and iTec to Danfoss DHP-iQ. Avoid inferring that current XTR has the same RS485 electrical role as ATEC.

Official XT/XTR Quick Guide specifically addresses **iTec XT indoor units and iTec XTR outdoor units**, supporting family continuity but not identical native protocol/firmware. Thermia international Online page explicitly lists **iTec XTR** as Thermia Connect supported; Belgian Online page lists iTec E / XT and omits XTR, apparently divergent regional listings. XTR product literature also advertises Online integration. This supports native integration being manufacturer-intended, **not that extracted Connect2.1.105 firmware is necessarily the actual compatible runtime or chosen CM930 schema**.
