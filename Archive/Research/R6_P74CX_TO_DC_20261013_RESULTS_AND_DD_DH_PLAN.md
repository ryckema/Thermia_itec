## Completed offline research — observed, conclusions and limits

### R6-P74CX — COMPLETE/POSITIVE for OTHER-frame subtype census; COMPLETE/INCONCLUSIVE for handshake cause
- **Baseline/hypothesis:** CT–CW six FC23-response-bearing Eco5 contexts plus one response-absent control; `OTHER` might hide an additional native `0x0F` transition or other distinguishing traffic. **Controlled change:** offline address/function/length/field reclassification only; original SHA-256/CRC and context bounds unchanged.
- **Observed genuine Eco5:** all 13 `OTHER` shape families resolved; no `0x0F` ADU in the `OTHER` class. In two slow contexts, `OTHER` counts 612 and 611; 102 `0x02 FC23` response-shaped frames each, 25 `0x06 FC23` request-shaped and 25 `0x0A FC23` request-shaped each. The `0x06` payload changes ~49.953 host seconds after the FC23 response, well before first `0x0F` ACK; `0x0A` one-frame transient follows, not precedes, ACK (+0.279/+0.310 seconds). `0x02` response payload within each slow pre-ACK window invariant.
- **Negative/unknown:** no hidden `0x0F` transition in that prior `OTHER` class. Other-address correlations do **not** prove native causal control, physical sender, XTR behavior or session authority. Tests: 25,031 original CRC-good frames, 7 source windows, 256 synthetic negative checks. **Abort/recovery:** source hash/CRC/bounds mismatch or authority promotion => reject offline result and rerun on originals; no device recovery.

### R6-P74CY — COMPLETE/POSITIVE offline FC16 retry reconstruction; cause INCONCLUSIVE
- **Baseline/hypothesis:** CX and CT–CW; changed first-page request image or a newly decoded frame might explain a late ACK. **Controlled change:** compare unchanged genuine Eco5 first-page FC16 request bodies and timing against original firmware-derived state definitions.
- **Observed genuine Eco5:** each of two slow Sep28 contexts has **102 same-content first-page requests, one unique image, 101 repeats**; first ACK shape after 108.653/108.507 host seconds from recorded FC23 response shape and 62/31 ms after the **last** request. Full broad 32/32 ACK-shape chain then follows in 34.441/35.905 seconds. A fast context's continuation may still pause; first ACK timing does not predict uninterrupted bootstrap.
- **Firmware-derived only:** CM930 `RESPONSE_SENT` after FC23 handler, `ACCEPTED` upon subsequent qualifying incoming FC16 (some addresses excepted); neither event is synonymous with wire short ACK or cloud application `OPERATION`. **Negative:** changing first-page contents not cause in those frames. **Unknown:** responder availability/physical slot/current XTR authority. **Abort/recovery:** same source-integrity criteria, no TX.

### R6-P74CZ — COMPLETE/POSITIVE for source-window timing; causality INCONCLUSIVE

Four response-bearing contexts recorded 29 FC23 requests before response; two early-response contexts three each. Gateway-absent control: 41 FC23 requests, no recorded native response or ACK over its bounded interval (reported >174 seconds).
- **STRONGLY SUPPORTED:** the historical Eco5 correlation is with post-gap host-time, not a fixed 108 s FC23-to-ACK delay. **NOT proven:** physical power-on timer, gateway startup cause, unique controller reset marker or universality. Restart provenance is collaborator documentation, not measured controller power rail. **Abort/recovery:** source mismatch, confounded intervention chronology => classify unknown, offline only.

## Evidence hierarchy and preserved boundaries

- **PROVEN locally, historical XTR:** guarded original V4.1 context-specific controlled restarts and 32/32/runtime (EXP355/386, S11F), bounded EXP387 semantics; not current V5 authority.
- **Observed genuine cross-model, NOT locally proven on XTR:** Eco5 118–120 s first-ACK cluster; identical 101 repeated first pages in two slow contexts, per-address `OTHER` traffic, status progression.
- **Firmware-derived only:** CM930 internal handshake/application states and request-image behavior. These states are not directly observable in contemporary CO retained captures.
- **OPEN:** G01 unique controller epoch, G02 exclusive work owner, G03 physical UART end-of-frame, G04 exclusive response slot, G05 electrical FIFO/DE drain and release (host-only partial), G06 DE fail-low at reset/brownout. CO historical early resync cause unknown. No genuine DCM available to user; no room sensor disconnection test.
- Native `0x0F` **TX HARD DENY** throughout. A standard FC16 address/count ACK is *not* semantic acceptance. A future new ACK or handshake attempt is a separate active experiment, not implied here.
